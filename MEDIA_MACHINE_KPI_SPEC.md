# AEROSFORGE Media Machine — KPI Specification

## North-star metric
**Qualified learning conversion:** the percentage of new audience members who move from a content touchpoint into an AEROSFORGE learning action (lesson start, quiz completion, Gauntlet attempt, or Pilot Passport activation).

Views alone are not the north star.

## Funnel metrics
| Stage | Primary KPI | Secondary signals |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | 3-second/initial retention, CTR | impressions, reach, returning viewers |
| Engagement | average percentage viewed | comments, shares, saves, repeat views |
| Education | lesson-start rate | quiz starts, completion, knowledge checks |
| Gamification | Gauntlet start/completion | replay rate, scenario accuracy |
| Identity | Pilot Passport activation | XP earned, streaks, level progression |
| Community | returning weekly users | discussions, replies, challenges |
| Instructor | CFI interest/lead conversion | verified instructor applications |
| Flight school | qualified lead conversion | discovery calls, inquiry-to-enrollment |
| Career | career-network activation | profile completion, employer interest |
| Revenue | free-to-paid conversion | ARPU, churn, LTV |

## Brutal rules
1. Do not celebrate viral reach if learning conversion collapses.
2. Do not scale a format with strong views but weak retention and zero downstream action.
3. Track each content asset with a unique asset ID.
4. Separate platform analytics from product analytics.
5. Never fabricate performance screenshots or testimonials.
6. Establish baseline data before setting aggressive targets.
7. Review weekly; kill weak formats quickly and double down on repeatable winners.

## Content score
Use a 100-point internal score:
- 25 audience retention
- 20 click-through/next-action rate
- 20 learning action rate
- 15 shares/saves/comments quality
- 10 return behavior
- 10 production efficiency

Safety, rights, factual accuracy, and originality are **hard gates**, not points. An unsafe or rights-uncleared asset scores zero for publication regardless of engagement potential.

## Experimentation
Every flagship package should have controlled variants for:
- hook
- title
- thumbnail concept
- first 15 seconds
- CTA
- scenario framing

Change one major variable at a time when practical. Preserve the winning learning objective even when packaging changes.
