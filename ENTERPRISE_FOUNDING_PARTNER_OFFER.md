# AEROSFORGE™ Founding Enterprise Partner

## Global Vertical Flight Network
**LEARN • FLY • EARN • ADVANCE • TRANSFORM**

AEROSFORGE is building a connected helicopter learning and career ecosystem: free education, AI-assisted study, scenario training, pilot development, instructor tools, flight-school pathways, and aviation careers.

## Founding-partner conversation
We are seeking a limited *operationally supportable* group of organizations to help validate the enterprise platform. We do not claim results that have not yet been proven.

### Best fit
- Helicopter operators
- Part 61 / Part 141 flight schools
- Aviation colleges and workforce programs
- Public-safety aviation organizations where appropriate
- Aviation education and training organizations
- Employers building helicopter-pilot pipelines

### Pilot modules
A scoped engagement can include:
1. Academy learning pathways
2. Scenario-based Gauntlet exercises
3. Rotor Coach learning support with safety guardrails
4. Pilot Passport progression roadmap
5. Instructor/CFI content tools
6. Cohort onboarding and progress reporting roadmap
7. Recruiting/career pathway design
8. Custom content planning subject to rights, factual, and aviation-safety review

### Safety boundary
AEROSFORGE educational content does not replace aircraft-specific procedures, approved manuals, qualified flight instruction, or regulatory requirements. Production aviation material passes factual, source-rights, safety, and human-review gates.

### Commercial structure to validate
Depending on scope, founding pilots may test project values in the **$25,000–$100,000+** range. This is a proposed commercial range, not a claim that customers have paid it. Final scope, price, deliverables, and terms are agreed with each organization.

### 30-day pilot concept
**Week 1:** stakeholder workshop + learner/problem definition  
**Week 2:** configured pathway + initial scenarios/content  
**Week 3:** cohort activation + feedback loop  
**Week 4:** outcome review + scale/no-scale decision

### What success means
Before engagement, select measurable outcomes such as:
- learner activation
- completion
- knowledge-check performance
- scenario participation
- instructor adoption
- qualified training/career leads
- administrative time saved, if measurable
