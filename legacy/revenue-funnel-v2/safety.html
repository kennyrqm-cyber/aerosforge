Aviation Safety Notice

AEROSFORGE ONE supports aviation learning, study, career development, and scenario discussion. It does not replace authoritative operational sources or qualified instruction.

Before flight

Use current FAA publications, applicable regulations, approved aircraft documents, operator procedures, weather and NOTAM information, and qualified instructor guidance as required for the operation.

AI-assisted content

AI-generated or AI-assisted material can be incomplete or wrong. Treat it as a study aid, verify safety-critical information against authoritative sources, and escalate uncertainty to an appropriately qualified instructor or operator.

Training scenarios

Scenario exercises are designed to improve judgment and discussion. They are not authorization to perform a maneuver, procedure, or operation.

Emergency information

For actual aircraft emergencies or abnormal procedures, follow the applicable approved aircraft procedures, training, ATC instructions when appropriate, and sound aeronautical decision-making.

AEROSFORGE ONE is building toward instructor-reviewed and source-linked educational content as the platform matures.
