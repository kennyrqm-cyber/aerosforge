FAQ

Launch-stage clarity.

Are the current checkout buttons live?

No. They are connected to the AEROSFORGE ONE Stripe sandbox. Test transactions do not move real money.

Is AEROSFORGE ONE an FAA certification provider?

No. The platform links to official resources and is building educational tools. FAA certificates, endorsements and approvals remain governed by the applicable regulations and authorized instructors or providers.

Can AI Coach answers be used as operational authority?

No. AI-assisted content must be verified against current authoritative sources and qualified instruction, especially for safety-critical decisions.

Can operators and schools partner with AEROSFORGE ONE?

Yes. Select “Enterprise / sponsor” in the priority-list form to enter the partner pipeline.
