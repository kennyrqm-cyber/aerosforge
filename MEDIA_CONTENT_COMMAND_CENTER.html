Production Pipeline

01 — SOURCE

Research authoritative references. Record source IDs and rights status.

02 — SYNTHESIZE

Understand the material, then independently structure the AEROSFORGE explanation.

03 — CREATE

Build original scripts, examples, scenarios, graphics, quizzes and challenges.

04 — REVIEW

Run originality, rights, factual, and aviation-safety checks.

05 — HUMAN APPROVE

No production asset reaches publication without human accountability.

06 — DISTRIBUTE

Adapt the approved core asset to each platform instead of blindly cross-posting.

🛡️ Rights & Originality Gate

GREEN — PUBLISH
Original AEROSFORGE work, verified public-domain material, or properly licensed material with documented terms.
YELLOW — REVIEW
Government/Creative Commons/third-party material where exact usage terms, attribution, or jurisdiction need verification.
RED — DO NOT PUBLISH
Unknown rights, copied text, copied diagrams, near-copy scripts, unlicensed media, or content designed to disguise plagiarism.
