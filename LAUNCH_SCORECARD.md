# AEROSFORGE™ Launch Scorecard

## Daily scoreboard

### Audience
- Reach
- Impressions
- Video views
- Average percentage viewed
- Returning viewers
- Subscriber/follower growth

### Learning
- Academy visitors
- Lesson starts
- Lesson completions
- Quiz starts/completions
- Gauntlet starts/completions
- Pilot Passport activations

### Commercial
- Qualified leads
- Calls booked
- Proposals sent
- Paid conversions
- Enterprise opportunities
- Partnership opportunities
- Revenue booked
- Cash collected
- Refunds/cancellations

### Efficiency
- Cost per qualified lead
- Revenue per visitor
- Revenue per learner
- Customer acquisition cost
- Average order value
- Conversion rate
- Sales-cycle length

## Brutal decision rules

**GREEN:** strong learning conversion + strong commercial conversion → scale.

**YELLOW:** strong attention + weak learning/commercial conversion → fix funnel.

**RED:** weak attention + weak conversion → stop spending production capacity until the premise is repaired.

**BLACK FLAG:** safety, rights, factual, privacy, or compliance problem → stop publication or sale until resolved.

## Founder rule
Never let a beautiful dashboard hide an ugly business.

Revenue must reconcile with actual transactions.
Leads must be real.
Customers must exist.
Claims must be supportable.
Analytics must come from source systems.
