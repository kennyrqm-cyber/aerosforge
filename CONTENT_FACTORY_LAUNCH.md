# AEROSFORGE™ Media Factory — Launch Sprint

## North-star
Build a recognizable aviation media brand while converting attention into free education, Gauntlet participation, Pilot Passport progress, and eventually instructor/flight-school pathways.

## Signature series
1. Can You Save the Helicopter?
2. 60-Second Helicopter School
3. The Gauntlet
4. Pilot or Passenger?
5. CFI Roasts Bad Pilot Decisions
6. Helicopter Myths
7. What Would You Do?
8. Aircraft Systems Explained
9. Would You Take This Flight?
10. From Zero to Helicopter Pilot

## Publishing flywheel
Short → long-form → free Academy lesson → Gauntlet → Pilot Passport → community → return.

## Editorial standard
No rage bait, fabricated emergencies, fake credentials, unsafe operational advice, copied scripts, unlicensed visuals, or AI-generated claims presented as verified fact.

## Launch cadence
Start with quality and consistency: one flagship lesson/week, 3–5 short-form cuts/week, one interactive Gauntlet/week, and daily community prompts as capacity permits. Increase only when review quality remains intact.
