## Seven-day attack cadence

### DAY 1 — PACKAGE
- Finish enterprise one-sheet and demo route.
- Define founding partner deliverables.
- Build CRM list of 100 high-fit organizations.
- Rank top 25 by urgency, strategic fit, and likely contract size.
- Publish a clear founding-partner CTA.

### DAY 2 — OUTBOUND
- Founder sends 50 highly targeted messages.
- Goal: 10–15 qualified conversations, not generic impressions.
- Publish flagship helicopter education content that proves product quality.
- Every piece routes to Academy → Gauntlet → Passport → commercial CTA.

### DAY 3 — DEMO
- Run live AEROSFORGE demonstrations.
- Ask prospects what would make a paid pilot undeniable.
- Send proposals the same day when requirements are clear.
- Track objections verbatim.

### DAY 4 — CONVERSION
- Rewrite offer around actual objections.
- Follow up every qualified prospect.
- Launch professional founding-member offer only when checkout/fulfillment is real.
- Publish proof of product, never invented customer proof.

### DAY 5 — PARTNERSHIP BLITZ
- Contact flight schools, operators, aviation programs, helicopter associations, and aligned brands.
- Push for paid pilots, signed LOIs where appropriate, or scheduled procurement steps.
- Do not count a vague conversation as revenue.

### DAY 6 — CLOSE
- Founder focuses on decision makers with active proposals.
- Remove friction: implementation plan, scope, timeline, deliverables, contract path.
- Never create fake deadlines.

### DAY 7 — ACCOUNT
Publish an internal truth table:
- cash collected
- signed booked revenue
- proposals outstanding
- qualified pipeline
- professional purchases
- refunds/cancellations
- conversion rate
- next action

If revenue is $0, say $0 internally and fix the offer. If it is $10K, learn. If it is $100K, compound. If it reaches $1M+, scale carefully.

## Qualification filter
A prospect is qualified only when we know:
1. Problem: what training/recruiting/education problem exists?
2. Authority: who can approve a pilot or purchase?
3. Value: what measurable outcome matters?
4. Timing: why act now?
5. Next step: what is scheduled and owned?

## Non-negotiable truth standard
Do not publish or imply:
- guaranteed income
- fake customer counts
- fake partner logos
- fake testimonials
- fake waitlists
- fabricated scarcity
- unsupported safety claims
- FAA approval that does not exist
- WINGS credit that AEROSFORGE does not actually award

**$1M is the attack target. Verified demand is the scoreboard. Trust is an asset we do not burn.**
