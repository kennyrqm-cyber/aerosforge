TRADEMARK / BRAND PREPARATION — NOT A LEGAL FILING

Working mark: AEROSFORGE
Potential related marks: AEROSFORGE Academy, AEROSFORGE Flight Academy, AEROSFORGE Rotor Coach, AEROSFORGE Pilot Passport.

Before filing:
1. Perform a comprehensive clearance search, including exact, similar, phonetic and design searches.
2. Review related goods/services, not just aviation-specific classes.
3. Decide the actual goods/services based on what is being offered now or in the near term.
4. File through USPTO Trademark Center after clearance and legal review.
5. Use ™/SM while claiming the mark; use ® only after federal registration for the registered goods/services.

The USPTO warns that a database search alone cannot guarantee registrability and recommends considering professional legal help for complex clearance work.
