# AEROSFORGE™ Content Integrity & Originality Standard

## Non-negotiable rule
AEROSFORGE creates original aviation education. We research authoritative sources; we do not copy protected expression, disguise copied work, or publish material with unclear rights.

## Research workflow
1. Identify authoritative sources.
2. Record each source in the Source & Rights Registry.
3. Extract facts and learning objectives—not paragraphs, scripts, diagrams, or distinctive expression for reuse.
4. Write an independently structured AEROSFORGE explanation.
5. Create original examples, scenarios, questions, graphics, and instructional sequencing whenever practical.
6. Cite sources where appropriate.
7. Run rights/originality, factual, and aviation-safety review before publication.
8. Require human approval for production aviation content.

## Prohibited workflows
- Rewriting copyrighted material to evade plagiarism detection.
- Copying another creator's script, course, diagram, animation, thumbnail, or distinctive explanation.
- Removing attribution from sourced material.
- Treating public availability as permission to reuse.
- Asking AI to produce a near-copy while hiding the source.
- Publishing rights-unknown images, video, audio, or graphics.

## Rights gate
**GREEN:** Original AEROSFORGE content; verified public-domain material; properly licensed material with documented terms.

**YELLOW:** Material requiring license, attribution, jurisdiction, government-use, Creative Commons, or other rights review.

**RED:** Unknown rights, copied/near-copy expression, unlicensed media, or content intended to conceal plagiarism. Do not publish.

## AI rules
Rotor Coach may explain, quiz, coach, and synthesize from authoritative sources. It must not reproduce copyrighted passages or help a user disguise copied work as original. AI-generated assets must be logged and human-reviewed before production publication.

## Academic integrity
AEROSFORGE teaches students to research, understand, cite, synthesize, and explain. It should not complete dishonest submissions or disguise plagiarism.

## Aviation safety
Copyright compliance never replaces safety review. Safety-critical content must be checked against current authoritative aviation sources and should clearly distinguish educational information from individualized flight instruction. AEROSFORGE is not a substitute for a qualified CFI.

## Ownership and evidence
Maintain creation dates, source IDs, licenses/permissions, contributor records, AI-generation records where applicable, reviewer status, and publication IDs. The goal is traceability—not merely passing a similarity detector.
