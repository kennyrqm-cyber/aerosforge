# Lesson 03 — Rotor Systems

## Editorial position
Make rotor systems visually understandable without pretending one design represents every helicopter. Distinguish main-rotor and antitorque systems, explain the purpose of rotor RPM management, and repeatedly point viewers back to aircraft-specific limitations and procedures.

## Master video
**Working title:** The Rotor Is the Engine of the Helicopter — But Not the Way You Think

**Hook:** The rotor is not simply a propeller pointed upward. It is an aerodynamic system whose lift, thrust direction, RPM, blade pitch, and control inputs are tied together.

**Core beats:**
1. Main rotor — primary source of aerodynamic lift/thrust in a helicopter.
2. Rotor RPM — designed to operate within aircraft-specific limits; power and rotor-system behavior must be understood together.
3. Blade pitch — collective changes blade pitch collectively; cyclic changes pitch through the rotor system in a way that changes the direction of rotor thrust.
4. Flapping and lead/lag — rotor designs accommodate blade motion; mechanisms vary by rotor system.
5. Antitorque — conventional tail rotors counter main-rotor torque and provide yaw control; NOT every helicopter uses a conventional tail rotor.
6. Rotor configurations — examples include teetering, articulated, rigid, coaxial, and NOTAR-type systems; teach the concept without implying identical handling.
7. Pilot discipline — rotor-system knowledge must be paired with the exact aircraft manual and instructor training.

## Short-form hooks
- “Why doesn’t the helicopter spin with the rotor?”
- “The rotor is doing more than making lift.”
- “Main rotor vs. tail rotor: different jobs.”
- “Rotor RPM is not a suggestion.”
- “One helicopter can teach you a rule that fails on another.”

## Gauntlet seed
A learner sees a helicopter configuration they have never flown. The challenge is to identify what is known from general rotorcraft knowledge and what must be verified from aircraft-specific documentation before making an operational decision.

## Quiz seeds
- What is the primary aerodynamic role of the main rotor?
- Why is rotor RPM management important?
- What problem does a conventional tail rotor help address?
- Why must rotor-system characteristics be treated as aircraft-specific?

## Oral-exam seed
“Explain how the rotor system produces lift and allows the pilot to change the helicopter’s flight path.”
