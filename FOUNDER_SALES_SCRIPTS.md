# AEROSFORGE™ Founder Sales Scripts

These are starting scripts. Personalize every message. Do not spam.

## Operator / enterprise opener
**Subject:** Building a stronger helicopter training pipeline

I’m building AEROSFORGE, a vertical-flight learning platform combining helicopter education, scenario-based decision training, AI-assisted study, instructor tools, and career pathways.

I’m speaking directly with a small number of aviation organizations to identify expensive training, learner-engagement, and pilot-pipeline problems worth solving through a paid founding pilot.

If improving one of those areas is on your 2026–27 roadmap, I’d like 20 minutes to show the working platform and understand your current process. If there is no measurable problem to solve, I will tell you that rather than force a fit.

## Flight-school opener
**Subject:** More qualified helicopter students—not just more clicks

AEROSFORGE is designed to move people from free helicopter education into scenario practice, a Pilot Passport, instructor discovery, and eventually real flight training.

I’m looking for flight-school partners who want to test a measurable student pipeline rather than buy generic advertising. I’d like to learn how you currently acquire and qualify students and show you the AEROSFORGE pathway.

Would a 20-minute founder demo be useful this week?

## Sponsor / aviation brand opener
**Subject:** Aviation education partnership with AEROSFORGE

AEROSFORGE is building a helicopter-first education and career media network. We are developing recurring educational series, scenario content, Academy lessons, and professional pilot pathways.

I’m exploring launch partnerships with brands that genuinely fit aviation education. Editorial and safety conclusions remain independent; sponsorship does not buy technical claims.

If that alignment fits your brand, I can send the founding partnership concept and audience/product roadmap.

## Discovery questions
- What is the most expensive training or learner-pipeline problem you are dealing with now?
- How do you measure it today?
- What happens if it stays unsolved for another year?
- Who besides you would evaluate a pilot?
- What would a 30-day proof need to demonstrate?
- Is there budget attached to solving it?
- What procurement/security/legal requirements should we know before proposing anything?

## Close
Based on what you described, I will send a scoped pilot with the problem, deliverables, success measures, timeline, responsibilities, and commercial terms. If those do not match what you told me, do not sign it—send it back and we will correct it.

## Follow-up
I’m following up because you identified **[specific problem]** as a priority. The next useful step is **[specific action]**. If that priority has changed, tell me and I’ll close the loop rather than fill your inbox.

## Rule
Never say “everyone wants this,” “spots are almost gone,” “we already have major partners,” or quote revenue/results unless each statement is verifiably true.
